## a11y-audit: U-031 "experienceCards.ts and ExperienceCardItem" (head fe08eb9)

Compared against the two components replaced (HomeCardItem on home, ResultCard in BrowseResults). Card, Card.module.css and Photo are unchanged by the diff.

### Blocking
None.

### Checked
- Headings (1.3.1, 2.4.6): browse cards stay h2, home Picks and This week stay h3; section h2s untouched.
- Link names (2.4.4, 4.1.2): bookable cards have one link, the title; meta, price and rating sit outside it. Guide-only cards have two sibling links (title, and "Visit host site (opens in a new tab)"), not nested.
- Images (1.1.1): the photo sits outside the link; alt is "" when decorative, else the alt text.
- Price read whole: `<p><span>{price}</span> <span>per person</span></p>` reads "Free per person" and "From $65 per person"; BookingPanel reads "Free per person"; BookingBar reads "Free" then "per person · next <date>". `fromPriceLabel(0)` returns "Free", never "From Free".
- Target size (2.5.8): the title link's `::after` covers the card; the host link is about 44 px tall.
- Focus (2.4.7, 2.4.11): ring drawn by `.card:has(.titleLink:focus-visible)`; not obscured.
- A card without a photo renders no image.

### Non-blocking
1. Horizontal layout at 320 px (This week): with a 96 px image and 24 px gutters the text column is about 138 px; `.price` is `white-space: nowrap` and the card `overflow: hidden`, so "From $65 per person" may clip. Pre-existing CSS (see H-87); worth measuring.
2. Accuracy, not WCAG: a card with an adult price of $0 and a child price of $5 reads "Free" (the PM ruling: the cheapest space-taking price decides). A product call for the owner.
3. Pre-existing: a non-decorative photo with null alt text silently becomes decorative.
4. Performance only: `cardPhotoSizes.horizontal` is 168 px while the image renders at 96 px.

### Unconfirmed
- Forced colors: the box-shadow ring drops; the card's transparent outline should become a system color. Needs a manual check.
- Reflow at 320 px for the horizontal layout (Non-blocking 1).
- Focus ring contrast (sky-700 light, sky-300 dark) against canvas and surface.
- Screen reader order of price and meta, assumed from markup.
