## a11y-audit: U-045b "Fake payment UI and event simulator" (head a1af608)

### Blocking
- `FakePaymentForm.tsx:204, 217, 228, 239`: all four card fields set `autoComplete="off"` (the `CardInput` prop type pins `'off'`); WCAG 1.3.5 asks for `cc-number`, `cc-exp`, `cc-csc`, `postal-code`. (PM ruling: kept `off` for the demo-only form, see DECISIONS 2026-10-29; not a round 2 item.)
- `FakePaymentForm.tsx:285-290`: "Fail verification" uses native `disabled={locked}` while "Complete verification" uses `loading`; clicking Fail disables the focused button and drops focus to `body`, and on the decline the sheet unmounts with focus not moved to the notice or Pay. WCAG 2.4.3. Fix: `loading` on Fail; move focus to the notice or Pay when the step returns to `entering`.
- `FakePaymentForm.tsx:321-326`: wallet "Cancel" unmounts the sheet and focus goes to `body`, not back to the wallet button that opened it. WCAG 2.4.3. Fix: return focus to the opening wallet button (or the cancelled notice), with a test.

### Non-blocking
- The sheets are inline sections, not dialogs (no trap, no Escape); fine for inline content.
- Nothing is announced while confirming; consider a `role="status"` "Confirming payment". The `lost` view's native `disabled` Pay drops focus.
- Contrast light and dark passes (secondary 7.8 and 8.8, primary 5.7 and 7.4, danger 7.7 and 9.8, focus ring 5.9 and 9.2, group border 4.1 and 5.1).
- Targets: inputs and wallet buttons 48 px, Button lg 44 px, test-card summary 44 px.
- Audit item: `.visuallyHidden` duplicated across component CSS modules.

### Unconfirmed
- 320 px reflow of the expiry/CVC/ZIP row and the test-card table not rendered.
- Dark-mode focus rings on the sheets not captured.
