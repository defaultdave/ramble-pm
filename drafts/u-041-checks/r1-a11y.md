## a11y-audit: U-041 "Booking bar, sheet, and panel" (head e2619c6)

### Blocking
None.

### Non-blocking
- `src/components/BookingChoices/BookingChoices.tsx` (about 95-125): the time slot buttons sit under an `h3` but are not grouped; wrap them in `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby` on the heading. `aria-pressed` toggles are an acceptable radio equivalent.
- `src/components/BookingBar/BookingBar.module.css` (`.bar`, sticky on a phone): no `scroll-padding-block-end` anywhere, so a focused control can sit under the bar (WCAG 2.4.11 risk, fails only if fully hidden). Add scroll padding equal to the bar's height.
- `src/components/DatePicker/DatePicker.module.css` (`.cell`): 44 px tall but about 39 px wide at 320 px; meets 2.5.8 (24 px), not the project's 44 px on width.
- Contrast from token values, all pass: secondary text 7.8:1 and 8.8:1; warning note 7.9:1 and 11.5:1; primary action 5.7:1 and 7.4:1; link 6.9:1 and 8.3:1; focus ring 6.4:1 and 9.2:1; control borders 4.1:1 and 5.1:1. Unavailable dates use `text-disabled` (4.1:1 light), exempt as inactive though they carry the availability signal.
- The dialog title and calendar month heading are level 2 from react-aria's Dialog context, the sheet's sections `h3`: an ordering nit.
