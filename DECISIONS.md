## Revisit after the Prototype

Choices kept for speed that later work may need to undo. Each is cheap now and costs more once many units copy it.

- **Single role per check** (U-116): actions allowed for "host, ops" (`cancelSession`, `publishWeek`) must call `requireRole` once per role until it accepts a list. Watch for copy-pasted `try`/`catch` pairs in data-layer modules.
