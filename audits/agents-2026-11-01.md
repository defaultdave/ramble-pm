## Tonight's experiments

### A1101-1 (type C, tooling): `tools/check-rounds.py` lists rounds and verdicts per unit for a day

- **Change:** new file `ramble-pm/tools/check-rounds.py` (134 lines). One line per unit whose verdict files were written in the day (local time, to 04:00 the next morning): rounds, each round's verdict per check, then a summary line with the count needing round 2 or later and round 1 failures by check. It reads the first 300 characters of each verdict file and understands all three fixture layouts (`r1-arch.md`; `architecture-audit.md`, `qa-agent-r2.md`, a plain file whose first line says "round 2"; `r1/architecture-audit.md` and a round folder holding only a README).
- **Metric and target:** the Step 1 job "units landed, round counts, which check failed each round" in one tool call under 2K characters of output, with the rounds right. Tonight by hand it took three calls and about 20K characters, one of them cut off at 120 lines.
- **Test:** 2026-11-01: 11 units, 7 needing round 2 or later, 1,660 characters; matches the manual listing, including the three units whose round 2 sits in plain-named files and whose round 1 is a README or was not kept. 2026-10-29 (baseline day): 43 unit folders in both layouts, 21 needing round 2 or later, round 1 failures arch 12, spec 11, review 8, qa 2, a11y 2; spot-checked against `u-050-checks` and `u-054-checks`. 2026-11-02: "No verdict files written". Caveat: selection is by file modification time, so a fresh clone loses the dates.
- **Result: passed.** Published with the night's commit. Revert: delete the file.

- **Finding:** three Opus PM chats held Figma reads and browser screenshots whole, re-read on every later turn: 2b4fd505 (10-31) 117K characters of `get_screenshot` and 30K of `get_metadata` at 237K average context; 7308837f (11-01) 79K of `use_figma` read results, 59K in one call; 447ccb2d (11-01) 121K of browser `computer` screenshots plus 59K of `browser_batch`, four of six screenshots at full scale.
