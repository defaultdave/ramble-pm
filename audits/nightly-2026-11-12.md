- The three that matter most: N1112-1, a moved booking never gets its reminder for the new date (moved later, the old reminder fires up to weeks early; moved earlier than the old reminder time, none is sent). N1112-8, an ops edit to a host's name or slug never invalidates the cache, so guests keep seeing the old host page. A signed-in header swap-in layout shift and the `?move` picker were not measured.
- Visual: 20 pages at 6 widths and both schemes (240 loads, all 200). No sideways scroll, no overlap and no clipping that hides content at any width; no dark-mode contrast failure on solid backgrounds. Four flags (menu against empty-state link on `/account`, capacity input and button on `/host/sessions`, a wrapped text-link line on `/ops/experiences/[id]`, visually hidden captions) were checked and are false positives.
