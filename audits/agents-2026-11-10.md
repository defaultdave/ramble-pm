## Tonight's experiments

### A1111-1 (type C, tooling): `day-cost.py` counts worktree subagents

- **Finding:** 11-09 finding 1. `token-audit` keeps the last three parts of a call's working directory and filters on that, so `ramble/.claude/worktrees/agent-*` (every builder and check launched with worktree isolation, and any chat that cds into one) loses "ramble" and is dropped. `day-cost.py` used the same filter. Yesterday it showed no `senior`, `qa` or `a11y-audit` at all, and 80 calls for 8cc1dcd1, whose main thread alone made 231.
- **Change:** `tools/day-cost.py`, after the module load: `audit.project_label = lambda cwd: cwd or "unknown"`, with a two-line why, and the docstring says the filter matches the whole working directory. 5 lines added, 1 removed, 1 file.
- **Metric and target:** per-type subagent calls equal the profiler's (which reads the transcripts directly); `interactive` calls within 5% of the profiler's main sessions; the session count does not change (no other project swept in); exit 0.
- **Test (control is the published script, treatment the changed one):**

- **Result:** passed. Every subagent type matches the profiler exactly on both days, `interactive` is within 0.3%, and no session was added. Published in tonight's ramble-pm publish. Revert: delete the three added lines after `loader.exec_module(audit)`.
