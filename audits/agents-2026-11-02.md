### A1102-2 (type C, tooling): the profiler sees chats opened at the workspace root

- **Finding:** the PM chat 8228229e ($68.35, the largest interactive session of the day) lives under `~/.claude/projects/-Users-davidfleming-Workspace/` because it was opened from the workspace root and moved into the ramble checkout. `tools/session-profile.py` globbed only `*ramble*` project folders, so it never reported that chat's context, compactions, polls or `STATUS.md` touches.
